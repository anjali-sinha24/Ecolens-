Our Purpose

Understanding Your Carbon Footprint

Understanding your carbon footprint shouldn't require complicated environmental data. EcoLens turns everyday lifestyle information into clear carbon estimates, helping users understand their impact and make more informed choices.

EcoLens calculates estimated emissions from activities such as transportation, electricity, household use, water, food, and air travel, making personal environmental impact easier to understand.
